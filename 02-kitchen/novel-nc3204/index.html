Ultra Slim Induction Cooker, EEL Grade 2

Induction Cooker · Kitchen

NC3204

Ultra slim touch induction cooker — 2000 W, 88.3 % thermal efficiency, 8 power levels and 8 temperature levels, 4-hour timer and 24-hour preset, with a pot included.

Quick reference — do this first

  • Never leave the cooker unattended while a pot is heating.
  • Use only flat-bottomed induction-compatible pots — a magnet must stick to the base.
  • Keep the area under and around the cooker clear of cloths, paper and flammable items.
  • Let the cooker cool before cleaning and wipe the glass top with a soft damp cloth only.
  • Use the 4-hour timer for long cooking, and the 24-hour preset for a delayed start.
