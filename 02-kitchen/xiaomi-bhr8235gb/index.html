Xiaomi Smart Air Fryer 4.5 L

Air Fryer · Kitchen

BHR8235GB / MAF14

4.5 L smart air fryer with a rotary control knob and display, app support, keep-warm, defrost, fermentation and 9 preset programmes.

Quick reference — do this first

  • Place on a stable, level surface with air circulation around the appliance — never against a wall.
  • Manual mode: press the control knob to enter manual mode, then turn it to the desired temperature.
  • Reset Wi-Fi: press the knob to enter “Reset Wi-Fi”, then turn to confirm.
  • Keep-warm is on by default — switch it off in settings if you do not want the food held warm.
  • Unplug before cleaning and never use water to rinse the control panel or the heating element.
