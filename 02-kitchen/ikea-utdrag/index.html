UTDRAG Built-in Extractor Hood, Stainless Steel

Extractor Hood · Kitchen

UTDRAG

Integrated 60 cm stainless steel cooker hood, supplied with a charcoal filter and a two-way ducting kit for recirculation or extraction.

Quick reference — do this first

  • Check the required clearance over the hob before fixing the hood — the mounting height above the hob is specified in the manual.
  • Charcoal filter is for recirculation mode. It must be replaced regularly, and more often if you cook a lot.
  • Extraction mode needs a duct to the outside with a minimum diameter as specified — check the manual.
  • Always switch off at the wall before cleaning or changing the lamp.
