MATÄLSKARE / MATTRADITION Microwave Oven, IKEA 300 Stainless Steel

Microwave Oven · Kitchen

MATÄLSKARE MATTRADITION

Built-in 1300 W microwave oven in IKEA 300 stainless steel, with 750 W microwave output, grill, weight-based defrost and a 60-minute timer.

Quick reference — do this first

  • Never operate empty — always put food or liquid in before switching on.
  • Stand clear of the oven when the oven starts. Do not let children use it without an adult present.
  • Stop / cancel: press the Stop button once to pause a running programme and twice to clear all settings.
  • Microwave only (no grill): use the metal-free containers supplied — never use metal trays or foil.
  • Forced air oven on the MATTRADITION variant can reach 250 °C — the door and outer surfaces get hot during use.
