131 L Single Door Direct Cool Refrigerator

Refrigerator · Kitchen

WF1D130

131 L single-door direct-cool fridge with a vegetable crisper, glass shelf and reversible door hinge. Knob control from 0 (off) to 7 (coldest); keep it at 4 for normal use.

Quick reference — do this first

  • First use: set the temperature knob to 4, connect and close the doors, then wait 2–3 hours (4 hours in hot weather) before loading food.
  • Normal setting: keep the knob at 4. Move between 4 and 7 for colder, between 1 and 4 for warmer. 0 switches the fridge off.
  • Careful with 7: setting 7 can drop the compartment below 0 °C and freeze your food.
  • After unplugging: wait at least 5 minutes before switching back on, or the fridge will not run.
  • Defrost: defrost manually when frost reaches 5 mm — empty it, unplug, open the doors, then wipe dry.
  • Vegetable crisper: stores fruit and vegetables while preserving moisture. Empty the compartment before lifting it out.
  • Long holiday: remove all food, switch off, defrost, clean, wipe dry and leave the door open.
  • Space to leave: at least 50 mm each side, 50 mm at the back and 100 mm above for ventilation.
