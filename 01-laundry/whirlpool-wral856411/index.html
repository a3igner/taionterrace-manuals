820 Pure Care+ Front Load Washer Dryer

Washer / Dryer · Laundry area

WRAL856411

8.5 kg wash / 6 kg dry, 1400 rpm spin, 15 wash & dry programmes including Steam Refresh and Drum Clean.

Quick reference — do this first

  • First use: run the DRUM CLEAN programme at 90 °C with detergent and no laundry before the first load.
  • Drum clean: run DRUM CLEAN every 40 wash cycles.
  • Pump filter: clean monthly — insert a coin in the groove to open the filter cover, unscrew anticlockwise, rinse, refit clockwise.
  • Inlet filter: clean the mesh filter at the tap every 6 months.
  • Automatic drying: press Dry until “A” appears on the display; or pick a custom drying time.
  • Delay: set a 1–24 hour delayed finish with the Delay button.
  • Eco cotton: the most efficient programme in terms of water and energy consumption.
