PEPPRIG Cleaning Set, Blue/Yellow

Cleaning Set (broom / scraper) · Utility / balcony

PEPPRIG (80567621)

Handy cleaning set with a push broom and a floor scraper with a rubber blade — for sweeping and washing floors, balconies and patios.

Quick reference — do this first

  • Assemble the handle before first use — the screw joint must be fully tightened.
  • Hang both pieces together on the wall to keep them together and save space.
  • Keep the rubber blade on the scraper — it is the part that actually wipes the floor dry.
  • Rinse and dry the broom head after use to stop dirt and soap residue building up.
