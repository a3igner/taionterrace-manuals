Mijia Vacuum Cleaner 4 C (Cordless Stick)

Vacuum Cleaner · Utility / store

BHR09BWGB / F205

Cordless stick vacuum with 170 AW suction, 400 W motor, an all-flooring electric brush bar, anti-tangle comb and a 75-minute battery.

Quick reference — do this first

  • Indoor use only, and never for liquids, wet rubbish, toner, ink or very fine dust such as plasterboard dust and fireplace ash.
  • Do not clean stairs without taking extra care for personal safety.
  • Use only the supplied power adapter, and never charge the unit near heat sources or in humid rooms.
  • Unplug the adapter before cleaning or maintenance, and never touch electrical parts with wet hands.
  • Keep the brush head away from people and pets while it is running.
  • Empty the dustbin and wash the filter regularly to keep suction strong.
