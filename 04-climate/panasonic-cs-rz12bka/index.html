Air Conditioner · Living room / bedroom

CS-RZ12BKA (CU-RZ12BKA indoor / CS-RZ12BKA outdoor)

1.5 HP R32 inverter split unit with Wi-Fi and AI control — 11,900 Btu/h cooling and 13,600 Btu/h heating, on Hong Kong Grade 1 energy label.

Quick reference — do this first

  • Read the operating instructions before first use — the manual covers both the indoor unit (CU-RZ9BKA / CU-RZ12BKA) and the outdoor unit (CS-RZ9BKA / CS-RZ12BKA).
  • This unit contains R32, a mildly flammable refrigerant. Never use mechanical means to speed up defrosting, and keep all work on the refrigerant circuit to qualified technicians.
  • R32 operates at higher pressure than R22 — if you ever replace the unit, the pipework and flare nuts must be replaced too.
  • Keep the indoor unit clear of furniture and curtains so the airflow is not blocked.
  • Let the unit run for 15 minutes before adjusting the temperature if the room feels wrong — the inverter unit may still be settling.
