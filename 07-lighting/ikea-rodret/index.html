RODRET Wireless Dimmer / Power Switch for Smart Lights

Smart Light Remote · Living room / bedroom

RODRET

Wireless dimmer and power switch for IKEA smart lights and plugs. Works standalone or with the DIRIGERA hub and the IKEA Home smart app. Pair up to 10 devices without a hub.

Quick reference — do this first

  • Pairing: press and hold the pairing button for at least 10 seconds until the LED blinks.
  • With DIRIGERA: install the IKEA Home smart app, then follow the on-screen steps to add the dimmer/switch to your system.
  • Without DIRIGERA: pair directly — up to 10 devices can be connected.
  • Battery warning: a blinking red LED when you press a button means the AAA/HR03 battery is nearly flat.
  • Use a LADDA rechargeable battery to reduce waste — the manual explicitly recommends it.
  • Risk of explosion if the battery is replaced with an incorrect type.
