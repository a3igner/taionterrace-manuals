50" QLED Q7F 4K Samsung Vision AI Smart TV

Smart TV (UHD/QLED) · Living room

QA50Q7FAAJXZK

50-inch QLED 4K smart TV with Samsung Vision AI. English and Traditional Chinese user guides are both provided, plus the setup and wall-mount guides.

Quick reference — do this first

  • Register the TV online at samsung.com/hk/support/product-registration to activate the warranty and be able to download apps.
  • Use the supplied One Remote and press the Home button to reach the Smart Hub / TV menu.
  • Let the TV acclimatise after moving it. If it was transported on its side, wait several hours before switching it on.
  • Update the software via Settings › Support › Software Update before connecting to the internet for the first time.
